Your GitHub work, every new tab.

Tabboard turns Chrome's new tab into your to-do list: tasks from GitHub Projects, pull requests that need you, and a standup you can paste in one click. It opens instantly.

Everything you check before you start.

Built for developers who live in GitHub Projects and would rather not open five tabs to know what's next.

✓Tasks by status

To do, in progress, review, blocked. Across every board and org you pick, with due dates on top.

⇅PRs that need you

Review requests, mentions and your own pull requests, with passing or failing checks at a glance.

¶One-click standup

What you shipped since yesterday, this week or any range. Copies with formatting for Teams, Slack or email.

✎Notes and links

Quick to-dos with due dates, plus production and staging links for every project.

◷World clocks

Your teammates' timezones with a working-hours dot. Search by city, PKT, EST or UTC+5.

★News and a little fun

Tech news for your topics, a contribution streak and a Friday "week in code". Switch it off if you like.

Three looks. Light and dark.

Brutal by default. Clay and Soft are one click away in Settings, and every look follows your system's light or dark mode.

Set up in a minute.

No account to create, nothing to configure on a server.

Install

Load it from GitHub in Chrome's extensions page. Chrome Web Store coming soon.

Sign in with GitHub

Enter a short code on github.com and approve. Or paste a personal token.

Pick your orgs

Choose which organizations to show. Your next new tab is your board.

Private by design.

Tabboard has no server. It talks to GitHub from your browser, and nothing about you or your work is collected.

Read the privacy policy
  • Your token, notes and settings stay in your browser.
  • It only talks to GitHub, plus the news sources you turn on.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads.
  • Open source, so you can check every line.
